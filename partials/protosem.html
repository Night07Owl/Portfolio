// BUILD LOG

Protosem

Weekly log of the prototype seminar — week 0 through week 20. Updated as the weeks happen.

WEEK 00

Getting to know each other!

We were introduced to the new environment and had a tour around Forge. Then a logical thinking game was played, where team collaboration was important and we also had fun! Over the following days we learned about ourselves, took the 16 personalities test, and found a relatable comic. Overall, Week 0 was really fun and we learned a lot!

WEEK 01

The 5S

We were separated into two teams and spent 3 days sorting the items in the shutter box. We learned what materials were there and started labeling them. As a team, we got to know each other better and learned that teamwork and patience are important. We even counted thousands of resistors in the end!

WEEK 02

Logical Thinking

Engaged in logical reasoning challenges, algorithmic puzzles, and structured problem solving. We learned how to break down complex engineering problems into modular steps and collaborate effectively to develop creative prototype solutions.

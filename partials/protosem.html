Protosem

Weekly log of the prototype seminar — week 0 through week 20. Updated as the weeks happen.

WEEK 00

Getting to know each other!

We were introduced to the new environment and had a tour around forge. Then a logical thinking game was played, where team collabration was important and we also had fun! Following days we learnt about ourselves, took the 16 personality test and then finding a relatable comic. Overall week 0 was really fun and we learnt alot!

WEEK 01

The 5'S

We were separated into two teams and then spent 3 days to sort the items in the shutter box. We learnt what all materials where there and started labeling them. We as a team got to know each other better and learnt teamwork and patience is important. We even kinda started counting thousands of resistors in the end!
